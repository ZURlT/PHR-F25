Pharmacy Course Guide

Explore course objectives, core concepts, and classifications for all courses in the Komar University of Science and Technology Pharmacy curriculum.

Department of Pharmacy — KUST

The Pharmacy program at Komar University of Science and Technology (KUST) is structured around four course tiers: Department Courses (Pharmacy core), College Courses (Medical and fundamental sciences), University Courses (General requirements), and Elective Courses.

Use this reference to review direct summaries of every course across all five years of the curriculum.

Medical Terminology

College Course Year 1

Teaches medical roots, prefixes, and suffixes to build fluently understood clinical vocabulary for healthcare settings.

Kurdology

University Course Year 1

Introduces Kurdish history, culture, language heritage, and social context.

Computer Skills

University Course Year 1

Covers basic computer operations, office software productivity, data management, and digital literacy skills.

General Human Anatomy

Department Course Year 1

Examines major structural systems, organs, and tissues of the human body.

General Chemistry & Lab

College Course Year 1

Covers atomic structure, chemical bonding, reactions, solutions, and practical laboratory techniques.

General Medical Biology

College Course Year 1

Focuses on cellular organization, genetics, and biological processes basic to medical and health sciences.

Medical Biophysics

College Course Year 1

Applies physics principles to biological systems, covering fluid dynamics, radiation, heat, and membrane biophysics.

Academic English I

University Course Year 1

Develops university-level reading comprehension, paragraph writing, vocabulary, and grammar skills.

Genetics

College Course Year 1

Explores gene inheritance, DNA replication, gene expression, and genetic variations in health and disease.

Academic Debate

University Course Year 1

Builds argument construction, public speaking, critical reasoning, and evidence-based debate techniques.

Academic English II

University Course Year 1

Advances academic essay writing, critical reading, research citation, and presentation skills.

Pharmacy Orientation & Calculation

Department Course Year 2

Introduces pharmacy practice standards, measurement units, dosage math, and dosage conversions.

Analytical Chemistry

College Course Year 2

Covers quantitative chemical analysis, titration, chemical equilibrium, and solution concentration methods.

General Histology

College Course Year 2

Studies microscopic structures of human body tissues and organ components.

Human Physiology

College Course Year 2

Explores functional mechanisms and regulatory processes of human organ systems.

Microbiology I

College Course Year 2

Introduces bacterial structure, microbial growth, laboratory staining, and fundamental microbiology concepts.

Organic Chemistry I

Department Course Year 2

Focuses on carbon structures, functional groups, stereochemistry, and organic reaction mechanisms.

Physical Pharmacy I

Department Course Year 2

Applies physical chemical principles to pharmaceutical solubility, phase equilibria, and drug dissolution.

Public Health Care & First Aid

Department Course Year 2

Covers public health principles, disease prevention, basic emergency care, and cardiopulmonary resuscitation basics.

Microbiology II

College Course Year 2

Examines systemic medical microbiology, viral and fungal pathogens, and clinical antimicrobial strategies.

Pharmacology I

Department Course Year 2

Introduces drug action principles, receptor binding, pharmacokinetics, and drug actions on organ systems.

Basic Immunology

College Course Year 2

Explores innate and adaptive immune responses, antibodies, cellular immunity, and immunological disorders.

Professional Communication

University Course Year 2

Develops interpersonal, written, and oral communication techniques for professional and clinical environments.

Organic Chemistry II

Department Course Year 3

Continues organic synthesis, complex functional group reactions, and spectroscopy applications in chemistry.

Physical Pharmacy II

Department Course Year 3

Covers disperse systems, colloids, rheology, surface phenomena, and stability of pharmaceutical dosage forms.

Pharmaceutical Compounding I

Department Course Year 3

Teaches formulation and preparation of non-sterile liquid and semi-solid dosage forms.

Drug Informatics

Department Course Year 3

Focuses on retrieving, evaluating, and applying medical drug information databases to clinical practice.

Pharmacy Practice Experience I

Department Course Year 3

Provides introductory practical exposure to community and hospital pharmacy operations.

General Pathology

College Course Year 3

Examines mechanisms of disease processes, cell injury, inflammation, tissue repair, and neoplasia.

Pharmacognosy I

Department Course Year 3

Studies natural drug sources, medicinal plants, natural product extraction, and bioactive constituents.

Biochemistry I

Department Course Year 3

Covers metabolic pathways of biomolecules including proteins, carbohydrates, lipids, and nucleic acids.

Medicinal Chemistry I

Department Course Year 3

Analyzes chemical structures of synthetic therapeutic agents and their structure-activity relationships.

Pharmaceutical Compounding II

Department Course Year 3

Advances compounding skills for specialized solid dosage forms, suppositories, and sterile preparations.

Pharmacology II

Department Course Year 3

Continues systemic pharmacology, covering cardiovascular, renal, central nervous system, and endocrine drugs.

Pharmacy Practice Experience II

Department Course Year 3

Expands practical clinical exposure, focusing on patient counseling and prescription processing skills.

Pharmacy Practical Training 1

Department Course Year 3

Offers structured site-based training in community pharmacy workflow and patient interaction.

Pathophysiology

Department Course Year 3

Connects basic physiology to altered physiological functions occurring in specific human diseases.

Biostatistics

College Course Year 3

Teaches statistical methods, probability, hypothesis testing, and data analysis used in health research.

Pharmacognosy II

Department Course Year 4

Focuses on advanced natural drug screening, phytomedicines, herbal formulations, and biosyntheses.

Biochemistry II

Department Course Year 4

Covers specialized clinical biochemistry, enzymatic regulation, signal transduction, and metabolic diseases.

Medicinal Chemistry II

Department Course Year 4

Explores synthetic therapeutic classes, target selectivity, metabolic pathways, and drug optimization.

Biopharmaceutics

Department Course Year 4

Studies how physical properties of drugs and dosage design influence absorption, bioavailability, and physiological response.

Pharmacology III

Department Course Year 4

Covers chemotherapy agents, antimicrobial therapies, immunopharmacology, and toxicological mechanisms.

Therapeutics I

Department Course Year 4

Applies clinical pharmacotherapy guidelines to plan individual patient care for cardiovascular and respiratory conditions.

Clinical Biochemistry

Department Course Year 4

Interprets clinical laboratory tests, blood biomarker levels, and metabolic indicators for medical diagnosis.

Toxicology

Department Course Year 4

Examines toxic substance exposure, poison mechanisms, risk management, and clinical antidote management.

Industrial Pharmacy I

Department Course Year 4

Covers industrial pharmaceutical processing, tablet compression, liquid filling, and manufacturing machinery operations.

Therapeutics II

Department Course Year 4

Focuses on clinical management of endocrine, gastrointestinal, renal, and infectious disease conditions.

Pharmacokinetics

Department Course Year 4

Calculates rates of drug absorption, distribution, metabolism, and elimination to optimize individual dosing schedules.

Cosmetics

Elective Course Year 4

Covers cosmetic formulation science, skin and hair care products, safety testing, and cosmetic regulations.

Pharmacy Practical Training 2

Department Course Year 4

Provides advanced practical training in patient record management, clinical interventions, and pharmacy operations.

Prodrugs

Department Course Year 4

Examines drug design strategies involving inactive precursors converted into active therapeutic agents inside the body.

Pharmacy Management & Economics

Department Course Year 4

Applies business principles, inventory control, financial budgeting, and pharmacoeconomic evaluations to pharmacy practice.

Research Methodology

University Course Year 4

Teaches scientific study design, data collection methodology, research ethics, and paper writing techniques.

Drug Discovery & Developments

Department Course Year 5

Explores phases of pipeline development, lead optimization, preclinical testing, and clinical drug trials.

Industrial Pharmacy II

Department Course Year 5

Covers advanced quality assurance, Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP), packaging technologies, and regulatory affairs.

Clinical Pharmacokinetics

Department Course Year 5

Applies therapeutic drug monitoring principles to adjust dosing for patients with impaired organ clearance.

Therapeutics III

Department Course Year 5

Covers complex therapeutic decision-making for oncology, neurological conditions, psychiatric disorders, and critical care patient management.

Graduation Project

University Course Year 5

Involves independent scientific research or clinical project work culminating in a written thesis and oral defense.

Drug Literature

Elective Course Year 5

Critically evaluates primary medical literature, clinical trial designs, and published pharmaceutical studies.

Hospital Training (Wards)

Department Course Year 5

Provides clinical patient ward rotations, participating in inpatient medical rounds and therapeutic plan reviews.

Hospital Training (Clinical Lab)

Department Course Year 5

Provides hands-on hospital laboratory rotations focusing on diagnostic testing procedures and clinical specimen analysis.

Advanced Drug Delivery Systems

Department Course Year 5

Explores targeted nanomedicine, liposomes, sustained-release technologies, and innovative bio-delivery systems.

Cancer Genetics & Personalized Medicine

Department Course Year 5

Examines molecular oncogenetics, targeted cancer therapies, pharmacogenomics, and tailored patient drug selection.

Pharmacy Ethics and Law

Department Course Year 5

Covers legal statutes governing pharmacy operations, controlled substance regulations, and professional ethical standards.

Advanced Pharmaceutical Analysis

Department Course Year 5

Applies modern analytical instrumentation, including HPLC, mass spectrometry, and spectroscopy, for quality control of medicines.
